1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled on the ServiceFlow AI marketing website (the “website”). The website is operated by legal business or operator name — to be provided (“we”, “us”), business address — to be provided.

ServiceFlow AI is a salon management workspace that is currently in development. This website is a marketing site: it presents information about the planned product and offers a Request a Demo form. The demo form is the only feature on this website through which you actively provide personal information, so this policy concentrates on it. The practices described here do not cover the ServiceFlow AI application itself; separate information will be published if and when the product becomes available.

2. Information we collect through the Request a Demo form

When you submit the Request a Demo form, the information you enter is:

  • Full name — so we know who to address in our reply.
  • Salon or business name — context for preparing a relevant walkthrough.
  • Email address — so we can respond to your request.
  • WhatsApp number — an alternative way to reach you about your request, used only with your separate WhatsApp consent (see section 13).
  • Country — to consider time zones, language and early-access availability.
  • Business type — to tailor the walkthrough to your kind of salon or studio.
  • Number of staff — to tailor the walkthrough to the size of your business.
  • Biggest salon-management challenge (optional) — to understand what matters to you.
  • Preferred contact method (Email, WhatsApp or Either) — how you would like us to follow up.
  • Privacy consent — the required confirmation that you agree to be contacted about your demo request and ServiceFlow AI early access.
  • WhatsApp contact consent — an optional, separate confirmation for follow-up by WhatsApp about your demo request.

Along with the values you enter, the submission records the date and time it was sent and a label identifying which website it came from.

Browser storage and cookies: the demo form does not save any of your entries in your browser (it does not use localStorage), and this website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

3. Why we collect this information

We use the information you submit only to:

  • prepare a product walkthrough that is relevant to your salon or studio;
  • arrange and carry out your demo, and discuss early-access next steps;
  • respond to you using the contact method you prefer; and
  • understand the type and size of business you run so our reply is useful.

We do not sell the information you submit, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to any newsletter or promotional list.

4. How demo requests are processed

When you submit the form, your browser sends the form values in an HTTPS POST request directly to Web3Forms, our form submission provider, which forwards them to our notification email address (see sections 5 and 6). A member of our team then reads the request and follows up with you.

ServiceFlow AI currently has no production backend, no CRM and no customer database. Demo requests are handled as individual email messages rather than being loaded into a product database or customer profile.

5. Our form submission provider: Web3Forms

The demo form is delivered through Web3Forms, a third-party form submission service. When you submit the form, your details are transmitted from your browser to Web3Forms’ servers before being delivered to our email. Web3Forms processes this data under its own privacy policy, which you can read here: https://web3forms.com/privacy. We selected Web3Forms to deliver submissions, but we do not operate or control its infrastructure.

6. Where your information is delivered

Submissions are delivered to the notification email address configured for our Web3Forms account. From that point on, your request is handled like normal email correspondence by our team and our email provider.

7. Web3Forms data retention

According to Web3Forms’ published privacy policy, submitted form data may be retained by Web3Forms for a period of up to three years. For the current and authoritative terms, see https://web3forms.com/privacy. We do not control how long Web3Forms retains submissions, and deletion of data held in its systems depends on the controls Web3Forms provides.

8. Email copies and correspondence

A copy of each submission, and any follow-up correspondence with you, may remain in our email inbox — and in any backups our email provider maintains — until we delete it. We do not commit to a fixed deletion period for these copies, and we will delete them when you ask us to (see section 11).

9. Website hosting and technical data

This website is hosted on Vercel. When you visit any page of the website, Vercel processes standard technical data — such as your IP address, browser and device information, the pages requested, and the time of the request — to deliver the site and for ordinary hosting purposes such as security and abuse prevention. Vercel processes this data under its own privacy policy, which you can read here: https://vercel.com/privacy.

10. International data processing

Web3Forms and Vercel operate infrastructure that may be located in various countries, and our team and email provider may also be located outside your country. As a result, information you submit or that is collected technically may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including countries with data protection rules that differ from yours.

11. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what demo request information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate; or
  • delete the information we hold about you.

To make a request, email us at privacy contact email — to be provided. Where it is reasonable to do so, we may ask you for details that help us verify your identity, such as the email address or WhatsApp number you used in your request. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

We will delete the copies we hold in our email inbox on request. Where your submission is also held by Web3Forms, we will take the steps available to us through our Web3Forms account to have it removed, noting that Web3Forms’ own retention policy (see section 7) and the controls it provides apply to its systems. Backup copies held by email providers may take longer to disappear.

12. Contacting us about privacy

If you have questions about this policy or about how your information is handled, contact us at privacy contact email — to be provided or by post at business address — to be provided. If you cannot reach us by email, you can also raise a question through the demo request form on the homepage.

13. How WhatsApp consent works

The demo form contains two separate consents, and they are not the same thing.

  • Privacy consent (required) — you agree that we may contact you about your demo request and ServiceFlow AI early access. Without this confirmation the form cannot be submitted.
  • WhatsApp contact consent (optional) — a separate checkbox in which you agree to be contacted by WhatsApp about your demo request.

The WhatsApp consent applies only to follow-up about your specific demo request — for example, arranging, rescheduling or confirming your walkthrough. It is not permission for promotional or marketing WhatsApp messages, product announcements, newsletters, or any contact unrelated to that request, and submitting the demo form without ticking it does not give us that permission.

If you select WhatsApp as your preferred contact method but do not tick the WhatsApp consent checkbox, we will follow up by email instead. You can withdraw your WhatsApp consent at any time by replying to our message or by emailing us at privacy contact email — to be provided.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website and ServiceFlow AI evolve. The “last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised, and the current version is always the one published on this website.
